Basic Help

No sound? Make sure your device volume is turned up and Silent Mode is off, then reopen the game.

Game not responding correctly? Fully close and reopen the app. If the issue continues, restart your device and try again.

Scores or personal bests? Your game progress is stored on your device. Removing app data or uninstalling the app may reset locally stored progress.

Contact Support

If you found a bug or need help, email us with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.
