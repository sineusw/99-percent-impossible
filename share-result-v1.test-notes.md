Manual acceptance for Share Result PR:

1. Ordinary non-PB result under 99%: no SHARE RESULT button.
2. First ordinary attempt (automatic initial PB) under 99%: no SHARE RESULT button.
3. New PB after attempt 1: SHARE RESULT appears below TRY AGAIN.
4. Any 99%+ Timer/Stop/Reaction result: SHARE RESULT appears.
5. TOO EARLY Reaction: no share button.
6. iPhone/Android with Web Share: button opens native share sheet with score, challenge name, and clean game URL.
7. Canceling native share leaves result modal unchanged.
8. Browser without Web Share: button says COPY RESULT and copies score + clean game URL.
9. TRY AGAIN remains primary and immediately resets the game.
10. No gameplay, scoring, voice, ad, Store, or payment behavior changes.
