Privacy Policy for 99% Impossible

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Petty Games Studios operates the game 99% Impossible. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

Information stored on your device

99% Impossible may store gameplay information and preferences locally on your device, such as best scores, streaks, selected voice settings, sound settings, and similar game state. This information is used to make the game function and remember your preferences.

iOS version

The current iOS version does not include an advertising SDK or offer in-app purchases. Core gameplay does not require an account. Scores and game preferences remain in local device storage.

Voice playback and interface fonts

For generated Petty voice commentary, the game sends the selected game-commentary text to a Vercel-hosted service, which requests speech audio from ElevenLabs. The game also requests interface fonts from Google Fonts. These providers receive the network information needed to handle those requests, such as the request's IP address and technical request metadata, under their own privacy and retention practices.

Android advertising and third-party services

The Android version may use third-party services provided by Google, including Google Play services, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob. These services may process information such as device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, approximate location derived from network information, and advertising-related data in accordance with Google's own policies and your device/account settings.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

If you make a purchase in the Android version of the game, payment processing is handled by Google Play. Petty Games Studios does not receive or store your full payment-card information.

Optional purchases on the website are processed by Stripe. The website uses a random browser buyer key and signed entitlement tokens to verify ownership. The current iOS version does not expose website checkout. Petty Games Studios does not receive or store full payment-card details.

Children's privacy

99% Impossible is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe personal information has been provided inappropriately, contact the developer at 99percentimpossiblegame@gmail.com.

Data sharing

Petty Games Studios does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate, distribute, monetize, secure, or diagnose the game, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Data processed by third-party services is subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact 99percentimpossiblegame@gmail.com or visit the support page.
